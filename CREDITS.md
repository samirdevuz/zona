# Credits

All models, textures and sounds in ZONA (M1–M5) are generated procedurally at runtime from primitives and
Web Audio oscillators/noise. No external assets are bundled yet. Models added through
`packages/client/public/assets/manifest.json` (see README.uz.md, section 6) must be listed here with their
source and license; original files go into `assets-src/`.
